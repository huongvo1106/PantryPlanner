# PantryPlanner
This project is a sophisticated tool designed to optimize your grocery shopping by intelligently analyzing your past consumption habits and suggesting grocery for the week.

✨ Key Features
Intelligent Prediction: Suggests optimal items for your next grocery trip based on the food types you regularly consume and the items you acquire each week.

Input Flexibility: Allows users to easily input or upload data regarding their usual diet and weekly shopping hauls.

Waste Reduction: By accurately predicting needs, the system helps prevent overbuying and the subsequent spoilage of perishable goods.

Personalized Insights: Provides a dynamic shopping list that adapts to your ongoing consumption patterns, ensuring you always have what you need on hand.

💡 How It Works
Input: You provide the system with two core pieces of data:

The food types you typically eat (e.g., chicken, pasta, spinach, apples).

The items you buy on a weekly basis (your current "stock").

Analysis: The underlying algorithm analyzes the difference and correlation between what you consume and what you stock, identifying gaps and predicting depletion rates.

Output: It generates a highly suggested shopping list for the upcoming week, ensuring a balanced and efficient inventory.
